Welcome to Cowdog Games

Cowdog Games is an independent game development studio focused on creating immersive and engaging gaming experiences. Our flagship title, "Deep in Enemy Territory," puts players in high-stakes tactical scenarios that test their strategy and decision-making skills.

Deep in Enemy Territory

Experience the tension and excitement of being behind enemy lines in our tactical action game. "Deep in Enemy Territory" challenges players to navigate hostile environments, gather intelligence, and complete missions while remaining undetected.

Key features include:

  • Immersive stealth gameplay
  • Tactical decision-making
  • Dynamic enemy AI
  • Multiple mission types
  • Upgradable equipment and skills

Coming soon to PC and major console platforms!
